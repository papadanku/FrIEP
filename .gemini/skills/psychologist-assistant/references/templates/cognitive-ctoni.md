
# Comprehensive Test of Nonverbal Intelligence · Second Edition

The Comprehensive Test of Nonverbal Intelligence · Second Edition (CTONI-2) is a popular, norm-referenced instrument that measures general intelligence through nonverbal problem-solving and reasoning tasks.

It is particularly useful for assessing individuals whose performance on traditional, language-heavy intelligence tests might be underestimated due to impairments or differences in language, hearing, or motor abilities. This includes people who are bilingual, non-English speaking, deaf, language-disordered, or have physical limitations.

The CTONI-2 is administered individually, typically takes about 60 minutes, and requires the examinee to simply point to their selected answer from a set of options, eliminating the need for oral responses, reading, writing, or object manipulation.

The six subtest scores are combined to form three primary composites, reported as Nonverbal IQs (Standard Scores with a Mean of 100 and a Standard Deviation of 15).

Full Scale: The Full Scale is the most comprehensive score, derived by combining the results of all six subtests (both Pictorial and Geometric). It represents the best overall estimate of the individual’s general nonverbal intellectual ability or fluid reasoning capacity.
