## B: How these factors affect the student's school performance and progress

## Template Examples for Logic Construction:

### Example: Limited English Proficiency

_firstname is an English Language Learner who currently receives specialized English Language (EL) support to facilitate language acquisition. While limited English proficiency can naturally interfere with the development of complex academic vocabulary and syntax, the team determined this is not the primary driver of _his/her current academic deficits. This conclusion is based on _firstname having received consistent English-based instruction since kindergarten and _his/her ability to communicate preferences primarily in English within the school setting.

### Example: Hearing/Vision Deficits

The evaluation team noted that _firstname has an identified vision impairment and requires corrective lenses. While visual acuity is necessary to access printed curriculum and board-based instruction, the team ruled this out as the primary cause of _his/her learning difficulties. _firstname consistently wears _his/her glasses during instruction, and _his/her teacher provides preferential seating near the point of instruction to ensure _he/she has full visual access to the general curriculum.

### Example: Environmental/Economic Disadvantages

The team considered the potential impact of environmental and economic disadvantages on _firstname’s functional performance. Although these factors are present in _his/her background and can occasionally limit access to supplemental educational resources outside of school, they are not believed to be the primary cause of _his/her lack of progress. _firstname maintains consistent school attendance and has had continuous access to Tier 1 and Tier 2 interventions within the school environment, yet _he/she continues to demonstrate significant delays compared to same-aged peers.

### Example: Lack of Appropriate Instruction

The team reviewed _firstname’s educational history to determine if a lack of appropriate instruction in reading or math contributed to _his/her current performance. Records indicate that _firstname has attended school regularly and has been provided with evidence-based core instruction. Despite receiving supplemental small-group interventions targeted at _his/her specific areas of weakness, _firstname has not made expected gains. Therefore, the team determined that a lack of instruction is not the primary factor impacting _his/her ability to progress in the general curriculum.
