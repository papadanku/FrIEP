## Why the Student was Referred

1. Triennial Evaluation: Use this when a student already receives services and is due for their three-year re-evaluation. Mention current eligibility areas.
2. SST/CST Referral: Use this when a school-based team (Student Study Team or Child Study Team) refers a student for an initial evaluation following interventions.
3. Parent Request: Use this when a parent or guardian formally requests an evaluation in writing.
